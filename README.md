## 📌 Executive Summary & Business Problem

* **The Brand:** Snabbit provides on-demand, instant househelp ($10\text{ Mins} \mid ₹99/\text{hr}$) across top Tier-1 Metros (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR, Pune, Hyderabad).
* **Current Challenge:** Operational supply (trained maids, cooks, dishwashers) is fully ready, but brand awareness is near zero.
* **The Opportunity:** The 6-month IPL season (March – August) creates peak domestic chore urgency (house parties, match screenings, guest hosting). 
* **Campaign Goal:** Deploy a **₹50 Lakh Google Ads media budget** to build top-of-mind recall, drive app installs, and lock in repeat $₹99$ trial bookings.

## 📊 Channel-Wise Budget Allocation & Bidding Matrix

| Google Ads Channel | Budget Split (% & ₹) | # of Campaigns | Bidding Strategy | Strategic Role in Snabbit's Funnel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Search Ads** | **30%** (₹15,000,000) | 4 Campaigns *(Brand, Urgent, Chore, Conquesting)* | **tCPA / Max Conversions** *(Max Clicks initially)* | Captures high-intent emergency chore queries at point of need. |
| **2. Performance Max (PMax)** | **25%** (₹12,500,000) | 5 Campaigns *(Geo-segmented Top Metros)* | **Max Conversions / tCPA** | Cross-channel AI scaling for app downloads & ₹99 bookings. |
| **3. YouTube Ads** | **20%** (₹10,000,000) | 3 Campaigns *(Bumper 6s, In-Stream, Shorts)* | **Target CPM / Target CPV** | Drives mass brand awareness & IPL match-day recall. |
| **4. Demand Gen** | **15%** (₹7,500,000) | 2 Campaigns *(Lookalike & Custom Intent)* | **tCPA / Max Clicks** | Thumb-stopping carousel ads on Discover, Gmail & Shorts feeds. |
| **5. Display Ads** | **5%** (₹2,500,000) | 2 Campaigns *(Cricket App & Retargeting)* | **vCPM / tCPA** | Visual presence on cricket apps (Cricbuzz) & cart retargeting. |
| **6. Shopping / Feed** | **5%** (₹2,500,000) | 1 Campaign *(Service SKU Feed)* | **Target ROAS / Max Clicks** | Promotes packaged hourly SKUs (₹99 Express Clean, Party Pack). |
